# CAPS v1 training stimulus and indices

## Reading the results

Strength and Conditioning estimate completed workout exposure in raw units. Axes overlap. CAPS plots historical Fatigue Index and Recovery Index from their respective source inputs. Progress keeps measured outcomes in their own graphs. Workloads, ranks and modeled deficits are engineering estimates. Each model uses version `caps-v1`. Missing observations stay unknown, observed zero stays numeric, and supported subsets name their method and coverage. All coefficients and cutoffs below are starting parameters for CAPS. Clinical, adaptation and performance prediction claims would require separate prospective validation.

`src/domain/training/metric.ts` defines common result fields including model, method, coverage, used and missing inputs, and reason codes. Personal percentiles use `100 × (below + 0.5 × equal) / N` with earlier compatible values. The pilot stimulus minimum is 10 comparable sessions if percentiles are introduced. The current stimulus views display raw results. Rounding happens at presentation.

## Conditioning

`src/conditioning/stimulus.ts` reads completed occurrences, active duration and optional actual observations. It retains work and rest seconds, bout order, rest type/intensity when recorded, timing, achieved output and its source. Calibrated metrics require matching modality and test identity. Assault Bike needs matching machine and technique. Elapsed or timer context is identified separately from measured output.

- **Aerobic endurance** shows same-mode domain minutes where calibrated or separately reported manual effort minutes. Duration and the longest continuous work are available independently of intensity.
- **Aerobic power** uses measured time at ≥90% of compatible VO₂max. Eligible sustained-hard work provides a separate fallback. The measured sustained-hard threshold defaults to a 60-second minimum screen for non-sprint work. Directly logged effort is shown under its own method.
- **Sustained high intensity / repeated effort** sums compatible cycling work above CP in kJ. Calibrated running can report meters above CS. Where measured calibration is absent, explicitly logged work and rest provide protocol exposure in seconds. Output retention remains separate. W′ reconstitution is outside this model.
- **Sprint quality** uses explicitly classified bouts up to 10 seconds and counts equivalent bouts at or above 90% of a prior best. Matched output or split timing has its own valid-bout coverage and current workout drop-off. Where only the protocol is available, CAPS shows short-bout exposure without a quality estimate.

Evidence for overlapping pathways, repeated output, measured oxygen uptake and machine specificity. [Running pathway overlap](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11194103/) · [Repeated cycling sprints](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8964751/) · [Interval oxygen uptake](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsc.12202) · [Assault Bike agreement](https://www.mdpi.com/1660-4601/18/16/8878).

## Strength

`src/strength/stimulus.ts` reads actually performed work sets. Plans, skipped sets and warm-ups are excluded. The completed editor can retain actual load, preceding rest, work time, range-of-motion notes, intent and a tested maximum. Catalog tags identify direct muscle attribution. The model accepts explicitly supported secondary tags. The currently bundled catalog has no separate secondary classification. Strength holds and repeated reps use separate units. Reported work and elapsed time determine work density where compatible. Volume-load is tracked in kg·reps for supported external loading.

- **Hypertrophy** sums direct involvement 1 and secondary involvement 0.5 times `clamp((6 − RIR) / 4, 0, 1)` for each muscle. RIR 0 is valid. Set exposure and RIR coverage explain missing or partial values. Load and exercise details give context to the effort-weighted units.
- **Maximal strength** weights completed reps by the square of the ratio of actual load to a previously tested, comparable 1RM. A newer heavier result can make the old reference stale. Lift practice stays visible when the reference cannot be used. Supported free weights have measurable external loads. Other resistance setups require compatible references.
- **Local muscular endurance** shows repeated-effort reps or active seconds per exercise/variant, with rest, density and continuous-bout context as available.
- **Power** counts performed reps that have explicit explosive or ballistic intent, labeled as exposure. Comparable measured rep power and velocity use their own units, and change in concentric velocity is reported separately. The current editor does not collect sensor rep streams.

Evidence for exercise specificity, hypertrophy over a range of loads, effort ratings and velocity loss. [Hypertrophy across loads](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27174923/) · [Failure versus 1–2 RIR](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38393985/) · [Velocity-loss intervention](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27038416/) · [Task specificity](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12436270/).

## Fatigue Index

`src/domain/training/fatigue.ts` computes personal recent training-burden rank. Load is completed minutes × observed session RPE (0–10). Home → Recovery → Training day log can fill missing session load, confirm that the day's workout log is complete, or confirm rest. The 14 preceding local dates have weights `2^(−daysBack/2)` for recent burden and `2^(−daysBack/7)` for accumulated context. A complete window and at least 14 of up to 60 earlier valid daily snapshots are required for rank. Date arithmetic is on calendar keys, independent of the length of a civil day. Same-day and future work are outside the anchor. Missing days or missing session fields appear as a known-load subtotal when there is some usable evidence. A new workout on a rest-confirmed day invalidates the rest assumption. Deleting the last workout on a complete-confirmed day leaves it unknown until rest is separately confirmed.

Only canonical Conditioning, Strength and Skills session records feed shared load. For timer-based Conditioning, active seconds are the completed timing stream excluding preparation and pauses, with inter-bout recovery included as tracked. A manually logged Conditioning elapsed duration can fill that field when observed active time is absent. Manually supplied whole-session minutes and RPE take precedence. Strength and Skills estimated durations and Skills' effort bands are not substituted for these measurements. The timeline row ID deduplicates canonical session records. As workouts are corrected or logged late, historical windows recalculate by completed local date. For Conditioning, reliable captured timezones place overnight sessions on the local day they ended. Frequency counts the previous 14 days, and time since the last known hard session uses an app-default hard RPE cutoff of 8. Observed run distance, same-machine bike work, calibrated exposures, and RIR-based hard sets stay in their own units and groups. Nutrition logging, sleep, perceived fatigue and soreness are context. Observed output loss compares a logged result to the preceding matched test in its own direction and unit. Test repeatability is not collected, so the UI makes no claim about measurement noise.

Evidence for the load channel and the limitations of projecting performance from training history. [Session RPE](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11708692/) · [Training model limitations](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16608765/) · [Model generalization](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-05392-8).

## Recovery Index

`src/recovery/recoveryIndex.ts` computes an equal-domain mean of available severity. An explicit current `0–10` readiness rating contributes `(10 − readiness)/10`. A personal sleep target contributes `min(1, mean(max(0, target hours − actual hours))/2)` across the latest night and available preceding two calendar nights. The latest night is required. A direct manual hours-asleep entry or an explicit sleep interval with minutes awake can support the same path. A manual entry for a wake date takes precedence over an imported night. Sleep schedules and unadjusted intervals are not observations of time asleep. Setting or clearing a target takes effect on the selected current date and leaves earlier targets intact.

Optional autonomic baselines use the previous 42 calendar days and at least 14 compatible observed days. RHR uses bpm and RMSSD uses natural log milliseconds. The scale is `max(1.4826 × MAD, floor)` with floors 1 bpm and 0.05 log units. Each departure is clamped to ±3. RHR scores only upward departures and RMSSD only downward departures. The domain averages available features. A composite needs check-in and one other domain. `100 × (1 − mean(severities))` is **Manual** if only manual inputs contribute and **Wearable assisted** when accepted device inputs contribute. Component sets, target, night coverage and source identities partition chart series. Existing native streams that lack documented protocol/window or device identity remain available as contextual readings where permission allows. HealthKit SDNN is incompatible with the RMSSD method. Device sleep is optional and bypassed when the athlete supplies a manual night. No vendor score, REM weighting or calorie estimate contributes.

Evidence for source consistency and the limited interpretation of sleep, HRV and fueling measurements. [Nocturnal wearable validation](https://doi.org/10.14814/phy2.70527) · [Sleep wearable validation](https://doi.org/10.3390/s24206532) · [Higher HRV during overreaching](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24136138/) · [Carbohydrate and glycogen replenishment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3132449/).

## Migration and data ownership

SQLite v13 adds `training_day_confirmations`, one confirmation per local date, and `training_session_loads`, one optional elapsed/RPE override per canonical timeline entry with cascade deletion. `recovery_sleep_targets` uses the effective date as its key and a null value to clear a target prospectively. `training_output_tests` stores measurements under a date/time, comparison identity and direction. Added nullable Strength set fields store actual details. The tested-maximum marker defaults to false for previous sets. The existing questionnaire JSON and revision tables accept the optional explicit readiness and sleep-hours fields. Conditioning's existing occurrence and snapshot JSON accept optional observed fields and dated calibration references. No new wearable integration or permission is required.

Former SRI/ORI code, stored scores and compatibility types remain for legacy reads. New charts and metric cards do not use their legacy scores. Former workout-emphasis snapshots remain in storage as well. Existing plans, sleeps and raw records keep their original meanings. No metric is reconstructed from an old numerical score. Device opt-out and existing permission/source choices control use of imported observations. The application is device-local.
